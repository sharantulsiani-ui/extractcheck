# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be direct, patient and specific. Challenge claims and methods without attacking
the person behind them. Make room for people who have less experience with the
tools, codebase or subject.

Do not harass, threaten, insult or expose another person's private information.
Do not use sexualised language or unwanted attention. Do not turn technical
disagreement into a campaign against a contributor.

## Project spaces

These rules apply in issues, pull requests, reviews and other public discussion
about ExtractCheck. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove material that breaks
them. Repeated or serious abuse may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

## Reporting a problem

If a public report would expose private information, use GitHub's private
vulnerability-reporting form. Say that the report concerns conduct. Do not
attach private documents, extracted content or unrelated personal data.

The maintainer will check the evidence and reply in private when possible. Any
public note will omit names and private details.
